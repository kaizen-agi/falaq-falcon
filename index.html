06 / 07

Questions before ownership

The practical details.
Answered clearly.

Do I legally own the falcon?

The exact ownership structure and your rights are set out in the agreement and supporting documentation before commitment.

Can I visit and handle my bird?

Private visits and supervised experiences may be arranged according to the falcon’s welfare, training programme and club terms.

What does the annual cost include?

The dossier itemises the agreed care, training, veterinary, transport, insurance and competition services.

Who decides when it competes?

Competition decisions follow the agreed programme, professional assessment, welfare priorities and applicable rules.

How are prizes, expenses and sale proceeds allocated?

All allocations and commissions are defined transparently in the individual ownership agreement.

What if it becomes ill or cannot compete?

Veterinary care, insurance where applicable, welfare decisions and financial responsibilities are addressed in the agreement.

Can I sell or transfer ownership?

Transfer and sale may be possible under the documented arrangements, applicable law and agreed process.
